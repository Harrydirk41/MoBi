3. Model & model choice

Distribution / permeability: Cellular partition coefficient method - Rodgers and Rowland, Cellular permeability - PK-Sim Standard
Processes: CYP2C19, CYP2C9, CYP3A4

The model is a whole-body PBPK structure with Rodgers & Rowland tissue partitioning and PK-Sim Standard cellular permeability. Rodgers & Rowland is the defensible choice for sildenafil because the molecule is a moderately lipophilic, weakly basic piperazine (basic pKa in the ~6–6.5 range) that carries partial positive charge at physiological pH; the R&R formalism explicitly separates neutral-lipid/neutral-phospholipid partitioning from electrostatic association of the ionized fraction with acidic phospholipids, which is the dominant driver of tissue uptake for bases in muscle and other high-AP tissues. Berezhkovskiy or Poulin-type methods, which treat distribution as lipid partitioning of the neutral species only, would systematically under-predict Vss for this compound class. PK-Sim Standard permeability is appropriate given sildenafil's high passive permeability and absence of evidence for uptake-transporter-limited tissue entry, so tissue kinetics are effectively perfusion-limited and no permeability-limited compartment is warranted. Elimination is represented as three saturable hepatic CYP pathways — CYP3A4, CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 — with recombinant-enzyme Vmax/Km taken from the literature and scaled to PK-Sim expression profiles. This mirrors the known biology: sildenafil is cleared almost entirely by oxidative metabolism (N-demethylation), predominantly CYP3A4 with a minor CYP2C9 contribution, and renal excretion of unchanged drug is negligible, so the absence of a renal clearance term is justified rather than an omission. Two structural simplifications should be stated plainly: gut-wall CYP3A4 is not separately parameterised beyond the standard expression profile, so oral first-pass extraction is carried implicitly by hepatic/intestinal expression and by the fitted kcat; and the pharmacologically active N-desmethyl metabolite is not tracked, which is acceptable for a parent-plasma objective but means the model cannot be used for metabolite exposure or for active-moiety DDI predictions.

4. Model equations (ODE system)

PK-Sim's standard human whole-body structure: ~15 perfused organs (lung, brain, heart, kidney, gut, spleen, pancreas, liver, stomach, muscle, skin, bone, adipose, gonads) plus arterial and venous blood pools. Each organ is further divided into plasma, blood-cell, interstitial and intracellular sub-compartments, so the solved system is one mass-balance ODE per sub-compartment (order ~10^2 states). The physiological volumes V and flows Q are fixed by the individual; only the drug terms below are model choices.

Perfused tissue (distribution)
V_t · dC_t/dt = Q_t · ( C_art − C_t / (K_p,t / R_bp) )
Each non-eliminating organ t (brain, heart, muscle, skin, adipose, bone, ...): rate in from arterial blood at flow Q_t, rate out with the venous effluent in equilibrium with tissue via the tissue:plasma partition coefficient K_p,t (R_bp = blood:plasma ratio).
Venous blood pool
V_ven · dC_ven/dt = Σ_t Q_t · C_t / (K_p,t / R_bp) − Q_co · C_ven + R_iv(t)
Collects the effluent of every organ; Q_co = cardiac output. IV doses enter here as an input rate R_iv(t) (bolus or infusion).
Arterial blood pool
V_art · dC_art/dt = Q_co · ( C_lung − C_art )
Blood is oxygenated/mixed through the lung and distributed to the organs.
Liver (hepatic metabolism)
V_liv · dC_liv/dt = Q_liv·C_art + Q_po·C_po − Q_hv·C_liv/(K_p,liv/R_bp) − CL_int · f_u · C_liv
The liver receives arterial + portal (Q_po, first-pass) inflow; CYP3A4 metabolism removes drug as a first-order intrinsic-clearance sink CL_int·f_u·C_liv on the unbound concentration.
Kidney (renal filtration)
V_kid · dC_kid/dt = Q_kid·(C_art − C_kid/(K_p,kid/R_bp)) − GFR · f_u · C_kid
Glomerular filtration removes unbound drug at rate GFR·f_u·C_kid. In THIS model this term is ZERO (GFR fraction = 0): renal excretion of unchanged drug is negligible, so the renal sink is switched off.
Oral absorption (PO doses)
dA_lum/dt = −P_int · SA_int · C_lum,u ;   J_abs = P_int · SA_int · C_lum,u  →  gut wall → portal vein → liver
Dissolved drug in the intestinal lumen permeates the mucosa (specific intestinal permeability P_int × surface area), enters the gut wall, and drains via the portal vein to the liver (first-pass).

Tissue partitioning. K_p,t is not a fitted number but is computed per organ from tissue composition by the Rodgers and Rowland method — a function of effective lipophilicity, the unbound fraction f_u, drug ionization (pKa) and each tissue's lipid / water / phospholipid / protein content.

Where the fitted / fixed parameters enter:

  • Lipophilicity — sets every tissue partition coefficient K_p,t (the distribution terms in all organ ODEs).
  • Fraction unbound (plasma, reference value) (fixed) — f_u — scales every unbound-driven term (K_p,t, CL_int, GFR).
  • Specific intestinal permeability (transcellular) (fixed) — P_int in the oral-absorption flux J_abs.
  • Permeability (fixed) — the cellular permeability P in the permeability-limited exchange term.

These are the governing equations; PK-Sim generates the fully instantiated system (every organ and sub-compartment, and the exact Rodgers and Rowland K_p formula) at build time and integrates it numerically. The concentrations plotted are the peripheral-venous-plasma state.

6. Pharmacological rationale

Two parameters were estimated. **Lipophilicity (2.4, sensitivity 1.00)** is by far the most influential parameter in this run and is therefore the one the data actually determine. However, it sits below the commonly cited experimental logP for sildenafil (~2.7–3.0); no logP/logD anchor was supplied in the literature table here, so the comparison is against external values rather than a provided reference, but the departure is real and should not be waved through. The downward shift is best read as an *effective* lipophilicity — a lumped descriptor that the optimizer used to reduce tissue partitioning and hence Vss — not as a measured partition coefficient. The direction of the mismatch is consistent with the IV diagnostics (IV bias 1.73, i.e. systematic over-prediction of IV concentrations), which is exactly the signature of distribution volume being trimmed to compensate elsewhere. Because sensitivity is maximal, this value is tightly determined *by this dataset*, which makes the discrepancy with measured logP a genuine model-adequacy flag rather than a benign uncertainty. **kcat@CYP3A4 (18.0, sensitivity 0.059)** is the opposite case: it is essentially unconstrained. The fit barely responds to it, and the returned value (18.00004) is numerically indistinguishable from its starting/bound value, so the optimizer effectively did not move it. It must be treated as a preliminary, weakly identified number despite the completed optimization, and it should not be quoted as a determined turnover rate — particularly since it is ~100-fold above the given in-vitro recombinant Vmax for CYP3A4 (0.169 pmol/min/pmol, Warrington 2000), a gap that silently absorbs IVIWE scaling and enzyme-abundance assumptions. Collinearity between lipophilicity and kcat@CYP3A4 is low (0.151), so the distribution/clearance split is not structurally confounded; the problem with kcat is insensitivity, not trade-off. Among the non-estimated parameters: **fu,plasma = 0.04** is fixed and agrees well with the literature ~96% plasma protein binding for sildenafil — this is real support, though as a fixed value it contributes no evidence from the fit itself. **Solubility 3.5 mg/L at reference pH 7.0** is fixed directly to Salerno 2021, and the CYP2C19/CYP2C9/CYP3A4 Km and in-vitro Vmax values are given from Warrington 2000 — all literature-anchored and not fitted. Three values require explicit caution because they are neither fitted nor literature-cited: **kcat@CYP2C9 = 1.0 and kcat@CYP2C19 = 1.0** are round placeholder-looking constants held at default, and since these minor pathways are not identified by the data, the resulting fractional metabolic contributions (fm) are assumptions, not results — this directly limits any CYP2C9/2C19 DDI or polymorphism prediction. Likewise, **specific intestinal permeability (8.6e-5 cm/min)** and **permeability (0.0014 cm/min)** are held fixed without a stated source; they govern oral absorption rate and tissue access and should be checked against in-vitro Caco-2/PAMPA data before the oral predictions are trusted.
