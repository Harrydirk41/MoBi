3. Model & model choice

Distribution / permeability: Cellular partition coefficient method - Rodgers and Rowland, Cellular permeability - PK-Sim Standard
Processes: CYP3A4

A whole-body PBPK model is appropriate for alfentanil given its systemic IV use, oral bioavailability data, and single dominant elimination pathway (hepatic CYP3A4, renal excretion negligible at <1%). The Rodgers & Rowland method for cellular partitioning is mechanistically justified: alfentanil is a weak base (pKa ~6.5) that partitions into tissues largely via ion-trapping and phospholipid binding rather than extensive tissue-specific transporter interactions, and R&R explicitly accounts for this via ionization state, logP/logD and plasma protein binding (here to alpha-1-acid glycoprotein, an acidic-drug-binding protein whose fu is captured as a model input rather than a partitioning determinant). Because alfentanil is not a P-glycoprotein substrate, PK-Sim's standard passive transcellular permeability model is a reasonable choice for both systemic cellular uptake and intestinal absorption, avoiding the need for active transport terms. CYP3A4-mediated hepatic clearance is the correct - indeed only required - elimination process, consistent with the known metabolic pathway; renal clearance is fixed to zero (GFR fraction = 0), which is the correct simplification given <1% unchanged renal excretion. Oral dosing is handled through the standard dissolution/solubility-permeability framework using the measured solubility and reference pH, with a separate intestinal transcellular permeability parameter distinguishing enterocyte crossing from whole-body cellular permeability. This is a defensible minimal-process structure: no additional transporters, extrahepatic metabolism, or nonlinear binding were invoked, and none are supported by the known biology.</model_choice> <parameter name="parameter_rationale">Lipophilicity was estimated at 1.93, below the literature logD(7.4) range of 2.1-2.2 (Baneyx 2014/Jansson 2008). Given the high sensitivity, the optimizer is using an effective lipophilicity below the measured value, most likely to compensate for how R&R-predicted tissue partitioning, together with the fixed permeability and estimated clearance, shapes the volume of distribution and terminal decline; this is a common effective-vs-measured lipophilicity trade-off and should be treated as a modeling compensation rather than a physicochemical finding. The remaining parameters (permeability, intestinal transcellular permeability, solubility, reference pH, molecular weight, GFR fraction) were fixed rather than fitted: molecular weight, solubility, and pH match literature/measured values directly, permeability values are PK-Sim/property-based predictions not subject to optimization, and GFR fraction = 0 correctly encodes negligible renal excretion.</parameter_rationale> <parameter name="conclusion">Overall model performance is good by the stated fit_verdict, with GMFE of 1.47, 86.2% of predictions within 2-fold of observations, and a modest overall bias (1.108, indicating a slight tendency toward over-prediction). 4. Model equations (ODE system)

PK-Sim's standard human whole-body structure: ~15 perfused organs (lung, brain, heart, kidney, gut, spleen, pancreas, liver, stomach, muscle, skin, bone, adipose, gonads) plus arterial and venous blood pools. Each organ is further divided into plasma, blood-cell, interstitial and intracellular sub-compartments, so the solved system is one mass-balance ODE per sub-compartment (order ~10^2 states). The physiological volumes V and flows Q are fixed by the individual; only the drug terms below are model choices.

Perfused tissue (distribution)
V_t · dC_t/dt = Q_t · ( C_art − C_t / (K_p,t / R_bp) )
Each non-eliminating organ t (brain, heart, muscle, skin, adipose, bone, ...): rate in from arterial blood at flow Q_t, rate out with the venous effluent in equilibrium with tissue via the tissue:plasma partition coefficient K_p,t (R_bp = blood:plasma ratio).
Venous blood pool
V_ven · dC_ven/dt = Σ_t Q_t · C_t / (K_p,t / R_bp) − Q_co · C_ven + R_iv(t)
Collects the effluent of every organ; Q_co = cardiac output. IV doses enter here as an input rate R_iv(t) (bolus or infusion).
Arterial blood pool
V_art · dC_art/dt = Q_co · ( C_lung − C_art )
Blood is oxygenated/mixed through the lung and distributed to the organs.
Liver (hepatic metabolism)
V_liv · dC_liv/dt = Q_liv·C_art + Q_po·C_po − Q_hv·C_liv/(K_p,liv/R_bp) − CL_int · f_u · C_liv
The liver receives arterial + portal (Q_po, first-pass) inflow; CYP3A4 metabolism removes drug as a first-order intrinsic-clearance sink CL_int·f_u·C_liv on the unbound concentration.
Kidney (renal filtration)
V_kid · dC_kid/dt = Q_kid·(C_art − C_kid/(K_p,kid/R_bp)) − GFR · f_u · C_kid
Glomerular filtration removes unbound drug at rate GFR·f_u·C_kid. In THIS model this term is ZERO (GFR fraction = 0): renal excretion of unchanged drug is negligible, so the renal sink is switched off.
Oral absorption (PO doses)
dA_lum/dt = −P_int · SA_int · C_lum,u ;   J_abs = P_int · SA_int · C_lum,u  →  gut wall → portal vein → liver
Dissolved drug in the intestinal lumen permeates the mucosa (specific intestinal permeability P_int × surface area), enters the gut wall, and drains via the portal vein to the liver (first-pass).

Tissue partitioning. K_p,t is not a fitted number but is computed per organ from tissue composition by the Rodgers and Rowland method — a function of effective lipophilicity, the unbound fraction f_u, drug ionization (pKa) and each tissue's lipid / water / phospholipid / protein content.

Where the fitted / fixed parameters enter:

  • Lipophilicity — sets every tissue partition coefficient K_p,t (the distribution terms in all organ ODEs).
  • Intrinsic clearance — the hepatic elimination sink CL_int in the liver ODE.
  • Fraction unbound (plasma, reference value) — f_u — scales every unbound-driven term (K_p,t, CL_int, GFR).
  • Permeability (fixed) — the cellular permeability P in the permeability-limited exchange term.
  • Specific intestinal permeability (transcellular) (fixed) — P_int in the oral-absorption flux J_abs.
  • GFR fraction (fixed) — the renal filtration sink GFR in the kidney ODE.

These are the governing equations; PK-Sim generates the fully instantiated system (every organ and sub-compartment, and the exact Rodgers and Rowland K_p formula) at build time and integrates it numerically. The concentrations plotted are the peripheral-venous-plasma state.
